What is NBI Clearance?

Official document issued by the National Bureau of Investigation certifying whether an individual has any pending criminal case or derogatory record. Essential for:

  • Employment applications (local/overseas)
  • Passport & Visa processing
  • Government transactions & permits
  • Business licensing & Travel abroad

🔄 NBI Renewal Process

Existing clearance holders may qualify for online renewal without returning to a branch — depending on previous record. However, if there is a 'Hit' or name match issue, biometrics re-verification might be required. Always check your NBI account dashboard.

⚠️ Common Delays & Solutions

  • Incorrect personal info → double-check before submit
  • Missing valid IDs → bring at least one primary ID
  • Payment verification glitch → keep digital receipt
  • “Hit” status (namesake) → requires manual verification (1-2 extra days)
  • Late arrival → strictly follow appointment schedule

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply without an appointment?

Most NBI clearance branches now strictly require an online appointment before visiting the site. Walk-ins are rarely accommodated except for special concerns. Always secure a confirmed schedule via the NBI portal.

How long does processing take?

Applicants with “No Hit” status typically get their clearance on the same day (within 1-2 hours). Those with a “Hit” due to name matches may undergo quality control, which could take 7-10 working days for final release.

Can first-time job seekers apply for free?

Yes! Under the First-Time Jobseekers Assistance Act (RA 11261), eligible applicants who are fresh graduates or first-time workers may avail of free NBI clearance, provided they present a Barangay Certification and other requirements. Check with your local PESO.

Is the NBI Clearance valid for 1 year?

NBI Clearance is typically valid for one year from the date of issue. However, some employers or agencies may require a clearance issued within the last 6 months. Always confirm validity with requesting entity.
