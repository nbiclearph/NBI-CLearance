Introduction

NBI Clearance Online Appointment — Philippines 2026

Applying for NBI Clearance in the Philippines has become easier through the online appointment system. Applicants can now register online, schedule appointments, complete payments digitally, and visit their selected branch for biometrics and verification.

The online process reduces waiting times and allows applicants to manage their clearance applications more efficiently. Whether you are applying for employment, travel, government transactions, or business requirements, this guide covers everything you need to know.

Overview

What is an NBI Clearance?

The NBI Clearance is an official document issued by the National Bureau of Investigation in the Philippines. It verifies whether an individual has any criminal records or pending cases.

Application Process

How to Apply for NBI Clearance Online — Step by Step

1

Create an Online Account

Visit nbiclear.ph and register using your active email address and mobile number. Provide accurate information to avoid delays during verification.

2

Verify via OTP

A 6-digit One-Time Password will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter it within 5 minutes to activate your account successfully.

3

Complete the Application Form

Fill out your full name, address, birth details, and identification information carefully. Double-check all entries before saving.

4

Schedule an Appointment

Choose your preferred NBI branch, appointment date, and available time slot. Book early — slots fill quickly especially in Metro Manila, Cebu, and Davao.

5

Pay the Processing Fee

Pay ₱155 total (₱130 clearance + ₱25 system fee) via GCash, Maya, LandBank PayGov, ECPay, 7-Eleven CLiQQ, Bayad Center, or Credit/Debit Card. Save your reference number.

6

Visit the Selected Branch

On your appointment day, bring two original valid IDs, your payment reference, and arrive at least 15 minutes early for biometrics and photo capture.

Renewal

NBI Clearance Renewal Process

Existing applicants may qualify for online renewal depending on their previous records and application history. Log in to your NBI account and select Renew Clearance. Some renewals may still require biometrics verification at the branch.

First-time job seekers may qualify for a free NBI Clearance under the First-Time Jobseekers Assistance Act (RA 11261), provided they submit the required barangay documents at the branch.

Common Issues

Common Reasons for Application Delays

  • Incorrect or mismatched personal information
  • Missing or invalid identification documents
  • Payment verification issues or failed transactions
  • NBI "Hit" status requiring additional verification
  • Late arrival at the scheduled appointment time

Pro Tips

Tips for Faster NBI Clearance Processing

  • Book your slot early — appointments open and fill within minutes
  • Choose smaller or provincial branches if Metro Manila is fully booked
  • Double-check all submitted information before saving
  • Bring original valid IDs plus at least 2 photocopies of each
  • Keep your payment reference number and receipt safe
  • Arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled time slot
  • Wear proper semi-formal attire for photo capture

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply without an appointment?

Most branches now require applicants to complete the online appointment process before visiting the office. Walk-ins are generally not accepted, except for senior citizens, PWDs, and pregnant women.

How long does processing take?

Applicants with No Hit status may receive their clearance on the same day. Those with a "Hit" may require additional verification taking several days.

Can first-time job seekers apply for free?

Yes. Eligible first-time job seekers may qualify for free processing under the First-Time Jobseekers Assistance Act (RA 11261). Required documents must be submitted at the branch.

What does "NBI Hit" mean?

A "Hit" means your name closely matches a record in the NBI database. This does not necessarily mean you have a criminal record — it may be a common name match and is usually resolved quickly.

How long is an NBI Clearance valid?

An NBI Clearance is generally valid for one (1) year from the date of issue.
